Redistribution obligations include a license copy, prominent modification notices, retained applicable notices and any applicable NOTICE attributions. The license text controls; this summary does not add restrictions.

Intentional submissions for inclusion follow Apache-2.0 and the [contribution policy](../CONTRIBUTING.md), using the [DCO 1.1](../DCO) attestation. There is no CLA or ownership transfer. The DCO is reproduced verbatim under its own permission notice.

## Separately scoped material

- App screenshots and promotional raster media under `docs/site/assets/` are subject to the [per-asset rights manifest](site/assets/RIGHTS.md), not the default Apache grant. Check the individual entry before reuse. Display permission does not license the depicted application's source code or grant training rights in depicted images.
- Names and logos are subject to [TRADEMARKS](../TRADEMARKS.md); Apache-2.0 does not provide a blanket trademark grant.
- System frameworks, optional runtimes, model weights and datasets have their own terms. See [the dependency inventory](DEPENDENCIES.md). No model-weight or dataset rights are granted merely by implementing an adapter.
- A shipping application, its private code, customer data and unrelated assets are outside this repository's license. An application screenshot is not a source-code release.

## Provenance and imports

This is a standalone reference implementation. The initial source review found no copied application source or vendored source from another photo-selection project. That finding describes the reviewed tree; it does not establish an employment or ownership determination for every potential contributor or authorize future imports.

Owner approval of Apache-2.0 establishes the selected licensing policy. It is not evidence that historical code from another project, employer, client or collaborator can be imported. Before accepting an import, record its origin, rights holder or submitter authority, license, modifications and required notices, and resolve any uncertainty with the relevant rights holder. Keep confidential evidence out of public issues. Review each release's actual included files, rather than assuming a prior approval covers additions.

Use only media you are authorized to process or publish. Public issue reports should use synthetic reproductions, never private-photo uploads.
